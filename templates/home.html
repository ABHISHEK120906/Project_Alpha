How FreelanceHub Works

Whether you're a client looking for talent or a freelancer seeking projects, the process is straightforward.

For Clients Hire
1
Post a Project
Describe your project, set a budget, deadline, and required skills.
2
Receive Applications
Verified freelancers submit proposals with their bid price and timeline.
3
Choose Your Freelancer
Review profiles, proposals, and verification badges to select the best fit.
4
Manage & Complete
Collaborate in the workspace, track progress, process payments, and complete the project.
For Freelancers Get Hired
1
Create Your Profile
Set up your professional profile with skills, experience, and portfolio.
2
Complete Verification
Verify your email, phone, identity, PAN, and payment account to become a Verified Freelancer.
3
Discover & Apply
Browse open projects, submit proposals, and showcase your expertise.
4
Work & Earn
Once accepted, collaborate with clients, deliver quality work, and receive payments.
