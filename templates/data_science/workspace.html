Data Leakage Protection & Modeling Standards
Chronological Validation

Time-series and predictive validation uses strict chronological train/test splitting (70% earliest records for training, 30% most recent for testing). Random K-fold cross-validation shuffling is deliberately avoided to eliminate future look-ahead data leakage.

Model Limitations

Predictions represent statistical estimates derived from historical patterns. External client approvals, scope creep, and unrecorded offline interactions may add unobserved variance. Always use models to guide decisions, not replace judgment.
